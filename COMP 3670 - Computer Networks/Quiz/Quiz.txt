Question 1: In packet-switched networks, queueing delay and packet loss may occur under certain conditions. When are packets most likely to be dropped at the router?  
Options: When packets are transmitted over fiber-optic links / When propagation speed exceeds the speed of light / When routers have unlimited memory buffers / When the packet arrival rate exceeds the transmission rate and router buffers become full  
Answer: When the packet arrival rate exceeds the transmission rate and router buffers become full

Question 2: We have explained the store-and-forward mechanism used in packet-switched networks. Which statement is CORRECT regarding store-and-forward operation?  
Options: The router must receive the complete packet before forwarding it onto the next link. / Store-and-forward only applies to wireless communication systems. / A router begins transmitting a packet before fully receiving it. / Packets are always stored at the router before forwarding it to the next router to guarantee it is not malicious.  
Answer: The router must receive the complete packet before forwarding it onto the next link.

Question 8: One major advantage of layering in networking systems is modularization. Which of the following BEST explains this advantage?

Question 9: In the host sending function, a host breaks an application-layer message into smaller chunks called packets before transmission. If a packet has length L bits and the transmission rate is R bits per second, which formula gives the transmission delay?
