About This Site

Welcome to our showcase of Windsor’s beautiful parks, libraries, and community spaces! This website was created to celebrate some of the city’s most cherished locations, places where families gather, friends meet, and the community comes to life.

Whether you’re planning a peaceful walk through Jackson Park, a sports day at Ford Test Track Park, or a quiet afternoon at Seminole or Riverside Library, each page gives you a closer look at what makes these spaces so special to Windsor residents. You’ll also find details about the WFCU Centre, the heart of local sports and events.

Take your time exploring, discover something new, and see how Windsor’s parks and public places continue to bring people together. We’re glad you’re here!
